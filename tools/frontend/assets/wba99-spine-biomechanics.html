🔸 Schober's Test (Lumbar Flexion)
📋 Procedure
1. Mark 10 cm above PSIS (L5 level)
2. Mark 5 cm below PSIS
3. Patient bends forward maximally
4. Re-measure distance
Normal: Increase ≥ 5 cm
--
Lumbar Flexion (Schober's)
🔸 Modified Schober's Test
📋 Procedure
1. Mark 15 cm above + 5 cm below PSIS
2. Total initial distance = 20 cm
3. Patient bends forward
Normal: Increase ≥ 6 cm (to 26+ cm)
--
Modified Schober's Increase
🔸 Fingertip-to-Floor Test
📋 Procedure
1. Patient standing, feet together
2. Bend forward, knees straight
3. Measure distance from fingertips to floor
Normal: 0 cm (touch floor)

Fingertip-to-Floor
🔹 Chest Expansion Test
📋 Procedure
1. Measure chest circumference at nipple line
2. Full expiration measurement
3. Full inspiration measurement
Normal: ≥ 5 cm expansion (young adults)
Reduced in: Ankylosing spondylitis
--
Chest Expansion
🔹 Ott's Sign (Thoracic Flexion)
📋 Procedure
1. Mark C7 spinous process
2. Mark 30 cm below C7
3. Patient bends forward
4. Re-measure distance
Normal: +2 to +4 cm increase
